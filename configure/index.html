    Ground bounds (viewer meters)
    Decision zones

    Zone geometry uses viewer meters after calibration: X runs east/west in the model, Z runs north/south, and elevation is viewer Y. Rectangle minimums must be smaller than maximums. Polygon vertices should follow the boundary without crossing.

    Solar analysis

    Choose Quick, Standard, or High exposure quality in the viewer. Sunlight hours are calculated from the model for each study date.

    Privacy and license preflight
